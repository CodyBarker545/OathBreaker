# Refined assassin source

The current hair has full scalp coverage, a layered golden-blond crown, outward tousled tufts, a long asymmetric fringe sweeping across one eye, and feathered side/nape layers. Fine strand ridges and packed longitudinal albedo/normal maps add detail. The dark fabric mask, layered outfit, hands and animation rig are preserved. `tools/update_assassin_tousled_hair.py` builds this revision from `backups/before_unhooded_hair/`. Earlier hood-related notes and scripts below describe preserved historical revisions.

Latest outfit reference: `layered_outfit_reference.png`. The current outfit has fitted blackened breast/abdominal plates, stepped pointed pauldrons, bronze rolled edges and rivets, wrapped tapered bracers, faceted knee guards, long ridged greaves and articulated boot plates. Crossed chest harnesses, two overlapping belts, utility pouches and pointed hip tassets sit over the continuous torso. A triangular burgundy cowl, long split sashes, torn back cloak and trailing shoulder scarf use the existing cloth bone chains, with faded ochre embroidery. Weapons are omitted. The corrected hands and current hood/mask/hair are retained. Studio previews use neutral lighting to show the dark steel and bronze materials.

`tools/match_assassin_outfit_reference.py` builds this outfit from `backups/before_layered_outfit/`. For manual follow-up edits, use the current Blender file. Earlier historical scripts below restore earlier outfit revisions if run. `tools/render_assassin_outfit_rear.py` produces the current rear view.

Latest hood/mask/hair reference: `hood_mask_hair_reference.png`. The current model uses a close pointed charcoal cowl with back panel seams and folded cloth, a darker fitted woven face wrap, and thin layered ash-blond fringe rooted beneath the hood. `reference_head_detail.png` and `reference_head_profile.png` show the front three-quarter and profile views. `tools/match_assassin_hood_reference.py` applies this revision from `backups/before_dark_hood_reference/`, preserving the corrected torso, hands and existing animation rig. Open `assassin_refined.blend` in Blender to edit the fitted Eliber body, armour, scarf, boots, materials and skeleton. Export `assassin_refined.glb` after editing. Runtime copy: `Assets/Characters/Assassin/Assassin.glb`.

The body originates from Eliber's Rigged Base Mesh, https://blendswap.com/blend/14675, CC0. The creator clarified the transition from CC-BY to CC0 in the listing comments. Original sources and licence records remain in `SourceAssets/characters/_base/`.

The model includes shaped boots, rigid armour, weighted cloth chains, packed material maps, and Idle, Walk and Arm_Motion_Test animation actions. Earlier exports and previews remain for reference. Draft animation may still contain sliding or intersections.

The detail pass follows `blonde_assassin_reference.png`, with the hood retained, a soft fabric mask restored at the user's latest request, and no weapons. It adds blond hair emerging from beneath a softened lined hood, a sculpted face beneath the cloth wrap, dished silver shoulder plates with rolled rims and rivets, brown leather straps, layered skirt panels, utility pouches, fitted fingerless gloves, laced boot shafts and a folded burgundy cape. A continuous tailored torso replaces the former separate oval chest, with blended spine weights through the waist. Human hands have fused palms/thenar pads, four jointed fingers, opposing thumbs and fingernails. The original rig and animation actions are retained and extended with `grip_` finger bones. Blender procedural bump adds surface grain; packed albedo images and modelled details transfer to GLB.

On the rig, `HandGrip.L` / `HandGrip.R` custom properties curl the fingers. Fit these values to future held objects and bake the Blender drivers into animation clips for game use. The model contains no weapons.

The latest face wrap is modelled as thin draped fabric with irregular folds, gathered cheek areas, rolled hems, fine hem stitching and tucked ties. Packed woven albedo and tangent normal maps, high roughness and a soft sheen create the cloth surface in Blender; the albedo and normal maps also export to GLB. `tools/add_assassin_cloth_mask.py` adds the wrap to the current source. The previous unmasked model is preserved under `backups/before_cloth_mask/`.

The underlying face and neck share a smooth skin surface; all eye-socket vertices are fully bound to the head bone. The tailored neckline is narrowed beneath the scarf to avoid the earlier chest/cloth intersections. This remains a detailed prototype; mesh optimisation and final animation polish are still required for production.
